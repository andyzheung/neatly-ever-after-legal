一句话总结：首发版本不收集、不上传或出售个人数据。你的游戏进度和设置保存在设备本机。

1. 我们收集哪些信息

我们不收集任何个人数据。本游戏没有账号、注册或登录功能，不要求姓名、邮箱、手机号或其他身份信息。首发版本不会向开发者服务器或第三方传输游戏数据。

2. 设备上的本地数据

关卡进度、星级、道具、设置和每日挑战状态仅保存在你设备上的应用容器中，用于让游戏正常工作。开发者无法访问这些本地数据。卸载 App 可能会删除这些本地数据。

3. 第三方服务与追踪

首发版本不包含广告、分析、崩溃统计或行为追踪 SDK，不使用广告标识符（IDFA），也不进行跨 App 或跨网站追踪。

4. 购买

首发版本不提供 App 内购买项目。若未来版本加入由 Apple App Store 处理的购买功能，我们会在该版本发布前更新本政策和 App Store 隐私说明。

5. 儿童隐私

本游戏的首发版本不收集任何个人数据，因此不会有意处理儿童个人信息。

6. 政策变更与联系

若本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页顶部的生效日期。有关隐私的问题，请发送邮件至 andyzheung@aliyun.com。

Privacy Policy

Neatly Ever After · Effective October 3, 2026

In short: The first release does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. Your game progress and settings remain on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal data. The game has no account, registration, or sign-in feature, and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or other identifying information. Its first release does not transmit game data to the developer or to third parties.

2. Local Data on Your Device

Level progress, stars, items, settings, and Daily Tidy state are stored only in the app container on your device so the game can work. The developer cannot access this local data. Deleting the app may delete it.

3. Third-Party Services and Tracking

The first release contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or behavioral-tracking SDKs. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across apps or websites.

4. Purchases

The first release offers no in-app purchases. If a future version introduces purchases handled by the Apple App Store, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure before that version is released.

5. Children's Privacy

Because the first release collects no personal data, it does not knowingly process children's personal information.

6. Changes and Contact

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Questions about privacy can be sent to andyzheung@aliyun.com.
